Full: a TelepromptScript (.tpmt) script, with @ 0:12 .. 0:20 windows, *emphasis*, / pauses and ((notes)). Each word is timed and marked as you read. A TELEPROMPTER.txt file (time and label on one line, text below) also works. Drop a script file on the page to load it.

Drag your face to move it. Double-click it to center it again.

The preview is mirrored like a mirror. The file is saved the way others see you, unless "Flip the recording too" is on. 4K at 30 fps saves about 40 Mbps, about 300 MB a minute.

Pausing the video also pauses the recording. Keys: Space or K play/pause, J/L back/forward 5 s, ←/→ blocks, R record, D download last take, M mirror, G grid, V circle/full view, T script on/off, H hide bar, S settings, F full screen.
